Net Plus, explained

What changed in 2026?

From September 2026, new rooftop systems connect under the Net Plus scheme. Here is what that means for you.

You sell every unit

All the power your panels make goes to the grid through a separate meter. CEB or LECO pays you a fixed rate per unit.

Fixed for up to 12 years

Your rate is fixed for the agreement period, so you know what you'll earn. Your normal electricity bill continues separately.

Want to use your own power?

A hybrid system with a battery lets you run your home on solar and keep the lights on during power cuts. We'll help you choose.

Before you apply

Check your transformer capacity from home

Rooftop solar can only connect if the transformer near your home has enough spare capacity. Until now, you only found out after applying.

Electricity Distribution Lanka (EDL), a successor company to the CEB, now lets you check this online for free before you apply.

Check transformer capacity ↗ Need help? We'll check it for you

Currently available only for transformers fitted with meters. EDL is installing meters on the remaining transformers. This is an official EDL service; Sky Power Solutions is not part of EDL.

FAQ

Questions we hear every day

How much does rooftop solar cost?

It depends on the system size, the panels and inverter, and your roof. In 2026, typical on-grid systems in Sri Lanka cost about Rs. 190,000 to Rs. 260,000 per kW. After the free site visit, we give you a fixed written quote.

Will my solar work during a power cut?

A normal on-grid system switches off during a power cut for safety. If you want power during outages, choose a hybrid system with a battery.

What roof types can you install on?

Tiled, sheet-metal and flat concrete roofs. Old or damaged roofs, and asbestos roofs, need an inspection first. We'll tell you honestly if your roof is suitable.

Can I pay with a loan?

Yes. Many banks offer solar loans. Use the loan check above to compare the installment with your solar income, and ask us about the documents you need.

How do I know if my area can connect solar?

Use the free EDL transformer capacity check above. If your transformer is not listed yet, or you are not sure, call us and we will help you check before you apply.

How much maintenance is needed?

Very little. Clean the panels every few months to remove dust. Hybrid systems also need occasional battery checks. Our team is one call away.
